# Privacy Policy for PetroTools

**Effective date:** 20 July 2026

Thank you for using PetroTools ("the app"), an engineering toolkit for
petroleum students. Your privacy is important. This policy explains what
information the app does and does not handle.

## Summary

PetroTools does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.
All calculations run entirely on your device. The app works fully offline
and requires no account, no sign-up, and no internet connection.

## Information We Collect

**None.** PetroTools does not collect any personal information or usage data.

Specifically, the app does not:

- Require you to create an account or provide any personal details.
- Collect your name, email address, location, or contact information.
- Track your activity or usage within the app.
- Use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.
- Access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.
- Send any data over the internet.

Any values you enter (such as pressures, flow rates, or reservoir data)
are processed locally on your device to perform calculations and are not
stored after you close the app, nor transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

PetroTools does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or
data-collection services.

## Data Storage

Because the app does not collect data, there is no personal data to store,
retain, or delete. Any input you provide exists only temporarily in the
app's memory while you use it.

## Children's Privacy

PetroTools does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children under the age of 13. The app is safe for use by students of all
ages.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be
reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the
app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
