## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an internet connection to use PetroTools?
No. All calculations run entirely on your device. The app works fully
offline and requires no account or sign-up.

### Does the app collect any of my data?
No. PetroTools does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or the
values you enter. See our Privacy Policy for details.

### I don't have the productivity index (PI). Can I still build an IPR curve?
Yes. Choose the "No PI — use well test" option and enter a well test point
(flow rate and bottomhole pressure) together with the bubble point pressure.
The app derives the productivity index and builds the full IPR curve for you,
showing every step.

### What methods does the IPR Calculator use?
It uses Vogel's equation and the composite (linear + Vogel) inflow model,
handling saturated reservoirs as well as tests taken above or below the
bubble point pressure. Each result includes a step-by-step worked solution.

### Why are my results shown with a "step by step" explanation?
PetroTools is built for learning. Every result includes the formula used,
the substitution with your actual numbers, and the final answer, so you can
follow and verify the method.

### Will more tools be added?
Yes. PetroTools is a growing toolkit. Planned additions include nodal
analysis (operating point from tubing performance), PVT correlations, and
decline curve analysis.

### How do I request a new feature or tool?
We'd love to hear your ideas. Send your suggestions to the email above —
user feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
